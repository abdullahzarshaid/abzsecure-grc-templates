How they connect

A practical first week

  1. Agree one business scope and assign a reviewer.
  2. Record the requirements you have rights to use in Assessment scope.
  3. Log missing proof in the Evidence register; keep file references private.
  4. Record material risks in the Risk register with an owner and an agreed scoring method.
  5. Give each gap a remediation owner and deadline in the Remediation tracker.
  6. Review the records and update decisions. A filled workbook is not evidence of compliance.
What these are not. The files contain no complete ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, Saudi or UAE requirement catalogue. They do not verify controls, run monitoring or certify compliance. Excel is the intended format; Google Sheets round trips are unverified. Add only content you have rights to use. Usage notes: TERMS.txt.
